The made-up part: assume someone above this token threshold is 100× to 10,000× as likely to join that leaderboard as someone below it. A log-uniform prior makes 1,000× the median assumption. These multipliers are judgment calls, not fitted evidence.

The actual equationp = sample users above / sample size
q = p / [bias × (1 − p) + p]
rank = 1 + floor((population − 1) × q)

A scenario median is not evidence of the most likely real-world rank. Total usage history and active-user population refer to different windows. The model doesn't resolve that mismatch.
